---
name: cad-roast
description: Convene the 4-agent Idea Roast Council (Believer, Skeptic, Investor, Judge) for commercial/startup ideas.
---

Execute the `cadence-roast` skill:
1. Capture the business or product idea.
2. Run the sequential council debate:
   - `roast-believer`: Max upside, who desperately needs this, why now, the one bet.
   - `roast-skeptic`: Who will not pay, workarounds, founder blind spot, fatal flaw.
   - `roast-investor`: Proof of willingness to pay, time to first dollar, 7-day smoke test.
   - `roast-judge`: Renders unambiguous verdict (BUILD, FIX FIRST, KILL).
3. Save the persistent ruling to an Antigravity UI Artifact.
